## Contribution decision

The present defensible output is an exploratory, reproducible diagnostic study: formal partial-premise predictability, exact controlled development factors, matched compact-model decision examples and explicit limits on corrective/mechanistic claims. This is not yet demonstrated to be a materially new international research contribution. A negative result is scientifically acceptable; a six-family generator and two-case pilot alone do not clear novelty or publication gates.

Highest-value pivot: test whether matched multimodal decisions gain beyond measured unimodal VLM baselines under the formal role ceiling, and whether No/U confusion persists after perception, answer-rule and nuisance controls. If that contrast is uninformative, prioritize a careful evaluation/replication report over claiming a novel mechanism or loss. GPU causal work and training remain separate future decisions. Preserve the university-approved title until the contributors agree on any external title change.
