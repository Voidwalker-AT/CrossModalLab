The new scientific value is an auditable explanation of why correct symbolic supervision and an apparently respectable score can still fail to establish joint evidence use. Dataset predictability is not proof that the model exploited the shortcut; intervention effects are not proof of a unique circuit; mean LL is not automatically a correction for sequence-length bias. These distinctions belong in the abstract/discussion if this remains the final evidence scope.

Maintain the October 28 deliverable as a stable university demonstration. For December, a credible negative result on controlled insufficiency with independent replication is realistic if resources and author review are available. A novel causal circuit plus a validated new training objective is substantially higher risk and must not be promised. If the mechanistic study cannot pass controls by November 20, narrow the title/contribution with the authors and submit only what the data support.
