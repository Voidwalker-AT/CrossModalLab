# Independent evaluation and statistical freeze

Status: protocol only; no independent/final records opened or evaluated. October 25, 2026.

The current six factorial families, previous 12 families, dashboard families, all training examples and every pilot case are exposed development. A custodian must create/retain new independent families and a documented exposure ledger. Different seeds alone do not establish independence. Keep related variants together; audit entity triples, exact hashes, templates, renderer regimes and perceptual identities without exposing closed labels to the model-selection process. The existing frozen final holdout remains separately permission-gated.

Before access, freeze code hashes, pinned model/quantization identities, extraction/prompt rules, content/answer order, parser, full-sequence decision rule, random seeds, complete endpoint/exclusion definitions and the central hypothesis. Primary behavioral endpoint: unsupported definite-answer rate on U families, with sufficient-case incorrect abstention as a required co-reported guard against always-U solutions. Report BA, macro-F1, class support/recall, invalid/strict-format counts and family counterfactual consistency. Generated completions and likelihood-scored answers are separate procedures and require separately counted compute. Cross-modal gain is paired multimodal BA minus the stronger matched single-modality baseline; oracle-role baselines are not substitutes for those model measurements.

Independence unit is the family. Use paired family-cluster bootstrap with B=2,000 for descriptive intervals, preserving model/input pairing. For a confirmatory comparison, preregister the practical effect, paired family statistic/test and alpha; use a prespecified Holm correction for a finite endpoint family, or an explicitly defined BH exploratory family for scans. Report every planned contrast, missing output and failure; do not drop a model's incorrect/invalid answer from the denominator. Hardware failures follow a frozen all-model family accounting policy, not post-outcome exclusions. Development intervals cannot be advertised as confirmatory significance.

Sample-size decision is pending scientific/compute agreement. A transparent conservative bound for independent family outcomes in [0,1] is `n >= ln(2/alpha)/(2 epsilon^2)` by Hoeffding. If stakeholders require 95% coverage within ±0.10, that bound gives 185 families; ±0.10 is an illustrative requested precision, not a pass threshold or chosen accuracy target. Eight logical variants/model/family and three candidate forwards require 8,880 forwards for two models, before unimodal/prompt controls. Observed per-candidate times project about 6.1 hours for Smol plus 0.7 hours for Qwen, excluding load/audit/session overhead. These are workload-transfer estimates, not authorization for an overnight campaign. A smaller precision/power design needs an explicit rationale and approval; six families cannot be relabeled adequately powered.

The next proposed 108-forward development screen is intentionally not independent confirmation. Publication gate remains NO-GO until independent evidence, human/rights checks where applicable, a defensible contribution and author/venue review are complete. No venue, acceptance or December submission is guaranteed.
