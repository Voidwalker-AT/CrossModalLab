## Objective audit

The frozen objective remains token CE + 0.25 ES with margin 1 and complete-answer summed LL. For insufficient evidence, ES is `max(0,1+max(sY,sN)-sU)`; for sufficient evidence it is `max(0,1+sU-sGold)`. At active, uniquely selected hinges, CPU autograd verifies score gradients `[1,0,-1]` and `[0,-1,1]` in explicit fixtures. These directions promote appropriate abstention and discourage incorrect abstention respectively; they do not establish useful parameter updates or generalization. The sufficient hinge does not itself penalize a wrong definite class; CE still carries that distinction.

The repository's function named counterfactual consistency is exactly the mean of two supervised CEs at temperature 1. A CPU equality test confirms this; it has no explicit cross-pair invariance constraint beyond shared training and paired labels. It should not be marketed as a novel consistency regularizer. Sequence sums differ in scale and token-length bias from token-averaged CE; candidate normalization changes decisions and cannot be retuned retrospectively against development outcomes. Numerical gradient correctness does not settle this statistical design issue.

The smallest justified next comparator is a known-premise symbolic verifier (already implemented), with a separately measured perceptual front end in any end-to-end system. The oracle's perfect logical accuracy assumes correct/exhaustive extracted premises and is not measured VLM improvement. No new training objective or optimizer is introduced. Existing CE50 versus ES5 is an unmatched-budget comparison; historical CE5 versus ES5 remains one-seed repeated-development evidence. Further matched multi-seed training is NO-GO until benchmark/perception and decision-rule controls justify its cost and the user approves optimizer steps.
