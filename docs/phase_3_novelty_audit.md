## Contribution decision

**A new benchmark:** UNVERIFIED. An oracle and three labels are not sufficient; the current v2 conditional-label shortcuts weaken a novelty claim. A counterbalanced, manually checked, independently evaluated task could support a narrow diagnostic contribution.

**A mechanistic finding:** NO-GO as a current claim. Existing probes are correlational; surviving intervention controls fail to isolate relation-specific effects. The new Qwen scores contain no neural capture.

**A new loss:** NO-GO as a current novelty/benefit claim. A signed margin, rejection-aware supervision and representation/action alignment have close prior art; our five-step pilot has no independent benefit evidence.

**A cross-model failure analysis:** the narrowest plausible research direction. Historical matched inputs show both models fail all six forks under their recorded semantic parsing, with meaningful output-format differences. This remains small reused development evidence, not an independent replication.

Recommended current wording: **an exploratory, reproducible diagnostic study of logical insufficiency, generator shortcuts and output-scoring confounds in compact VLMs**. The software is mature; peer-reviewed contribution readiness remains conditional on stronger experiments. By December 1, prioritize independent behavioral replication and one valid narrow causal study only if controls pass. A well-controlled negative result is preferable to an unsupported mechanism or forced loss novelty. Verify subsequent work and full methods before any priority/“first” claim.
