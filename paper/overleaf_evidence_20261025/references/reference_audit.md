# Reference audit

The exact eight-page Overleaf source/PDF and its asserted fifteen-entry bibliography are unavailable. The repository draft actually lists W1–W24. All 24 primary records were reopened online in this mission. LoRA and the two official model cards were added because they directly support implementation identities, not to pad the bibliography. The 27-entry BibTeX is an evidence library, not a recommendation to cite every entry.

Entries use the verified arXiv-version metadata when proceedings normalization is uncertain. Preprint years therefore differ from conference years, e.g. LoRA 2021 and IOI 2022. This is intentional. OpenReview's IOI landing page returned a browser-verification challenge; arXiv supplied title/authors. W11 deliberately follows arXiv v7 author order, not a claim about ICCV's proceedings author order. Model cards are official documentation, not peer-reviewed studies.

Fresh inspection generally establishes title, authors, identifier and abstract-level relevance. It does not certify every experimental number or full methodological novelty. The existing 21-paper selected-methods/results audit is copied under audit/source_records and its page/table locators remain explicit. Reliable VQA sections 3–5/appendices and activation-patching sections 2–6 were additionally opened in HTML. No foreign full paper was copied into this sharing package. No literature experiment was reproduced. Unresolved full-text, venue-normalization and novelty questions remain UNVERIFIED.
