## Legitimate role in the university paper

Smol contributes an earlier behavioral failure battery, verified sparse activation artifacts and exploratory linear representation analysis. Qwen contributes the later development checkpoint/format study and evidence-aware QLoRA feasibility. These are complementary evidence streams, not interchangeable measurements on one model. A tiny matched candidate pilot provides limited common-input observations; it does not make the two larger behavioral datasets comparable.

Include Smol's 18/36 descriptive behavior and class counts, the four-layer probe plot with selection/CI caveats, and the explicit absence of causal attribution. No Smol training or Smol adapter result is evidenced by the inspected research inventory; do not transfer Qwen training claims to Smol. Further Smol GPU execution is unnecessary for an accurate preliminary university paper. The strongest claim is reproducible separation of logical evidence, observed model behavior and correlational representations, not discovery of an internal failure mechanism.
